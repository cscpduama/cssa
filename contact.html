How do I join CSSA?
+

All Computer Science department students at PDUAM Amjonga are automatically members. You can participate in all our activities, bootcamps, and projects by attending our meetings or joining our online group.

Events
What events does CSSA host?
+

We organize beginner-friendly workshops (like Git, web development, and Python), annual coding sprints, hackathons, and seminars led by system developers and tech alumni.

AcademicsProjects
Can first-year students join a project team?
+

Absolutely! We encourage first-semester students to participate. We pair beginners with senior mentors so you can learn practical coding workflows and Git collaborations on real-world projects.

Membership
Is there a membership fee?
+

No, there is no membership fee. All CSSA workshops, resource files, coding contests, and events are completely free of charge for students enrolled in the CS department at PDUAM.

How do I suggest a workshop topic?
+

We love student suggestions! You can pitch workshop ideas or request tutorial topics by filling out our contact form below or speaking directly with any of the Core Committee members.
