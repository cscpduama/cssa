About

What we stand for

We give CS students at PDUAM a place to go deeper than the syllabus — through peer learning, real projects, and a community that shows up for each other.

CS
Department of Computer Science
CSSA is the official student association of the CS Department at PDUAM, part of a growing academic community in Amjonga, Assam.

The CSSA has grown into the hub for Computer Science life at PDUAM — a space where coursework turns into curiosity, and curiosity turns into projects worth showing off. Members organise workshops, coding contests, and talks that connect classroom fundamentals to the tools the industry actually uses.

From first-year orientation to final-year placement prep, CSSA stays involved throughout the CS journey — building a department that supports its students long after they graduate.

1

Support New Students

Mentor first-year students through orientation, study groups, and course guidance.

5

Celebrate Achievement

Showcase projects, research, and contributions from students across the department.

6

Strengthen the Network

Keep alumni, faculty, and current students connected long after graduation.

Strong Bonds

Campus rooted, industry connected

From Amjonga to the wider tech world, CSSA is shaping how PDUAM's Computer Science students learn, build, and launch their careers. Our network spans open source, software development, research, and entrepreneurship — powered by students who lead with curiosity.

We're bound by more than a department — we're bound by a shared commitment to building skills that travel. For every student who comes next, CSSA is a launchpad, a community, and a habit worth keeping.
